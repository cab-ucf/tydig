# NIH MIRA application (R35, NIGMS)

A Typst project for a Maximizing Investigators' Research Award: funding for
a research program, not a project (the NOFO is `nofo:` in `grant.yaml`). It is
edited live by people in tydig while you work, so keep each change small and
focused.

## Where things are

- `grant.yaml`: every fact stated more than once (title, the PD/PI, effort,
  the program's `directions:`, letters). Change a fact here, never in the
  `.typ` files.
- **Never write "aims"**, anywhere: a MIRA that does is withdrawn before
  review. It shows red where it appears. Say directions, questions, goals.
- `main.typ`: the Research Strategy (6 pages) and References in one
  document. There is no Specific Aims page. Its headings are the NOFO's,
  in place of Significance, Innovation and Approach: Background, Recent
  progress (the past five years), Overview of future research plans (key
  questions and general strategies, no detailed experiments; each direction's
  share of effort, adding to 100). `make` cuts it at `references`.
- `#todo[...]` (grey) says what a passage must cover. Replace it with the
  text; never leave one in a finished section.
- The abstract (`summary.typ`): the laboratory's research, its goals for
  the next five years, and the program's overall vision; no aims there
  either.
- The biosketch (made in SciENcv, uploaded into `docs/`): the PD/PI's only,
  and of collaborators the MIRA pays. Tailor the personal statement to the
  MIRA (mentoring, and service to the community beyond what the institution
  expects; no person-months or dollars for listed projects); contributions
  to science from the past five years, without figures or preliminary data.
- A renewal (`mechanism.renewal: true`, established MIRA): Recent progress is
  the current MIRA's; reviewers weigh it.
- `summary.typ`, `narrative.typ`, `budget.typ`, `resources.typ`,
  `equipment.typ`, `dmsp.typ`, `letters.typ`: one NIH attachment each.
- `lib/nih.typ`: NIH formatting; `lib/grant.typ`: the `section`, `letter`
  and `todo` helpers.
  `lib/brand.typ`: colours and letterhead.
- `refs.bib`: references; cite them as `@key` in `main.typ` only.
- `package.yaml`: every component of the application, in NIH's order, and
  where each comes from (built here, uploaded into `docs/`, or an online
  form). The Checklist panel and `make full` read it. Add or drop items to
  match the funding opportunity.
- `budget.yaml`: what is requested each year (the R&R budget is not
  itemised: the PD/PI at $0, the request in Section F), plus equipment and
  data management and sharing costs, which `budget.typ` justifies.
  `grant.yaml` `effort:` must put at least the NOFO's share of research
  effort on the MIRA, or `budget.typ` warns in red; `lib/budget.typ` warns
  over the cap, or where a list is not one entry a year.
- `out/` (built PDFs) and `build/`: generated by `make`; never edit or commit them.

## Rules

- Paths start with `/` (`image("/assets/x.svg")`). A relative path resolves
  from the file that contains it, not from the document being compiled.
- Page limits come from `limits:` in `grant.yaml`. A section over its limit
  shows a red warning on its first page: shorten the text, never the
  formatting.
- NIH format: 11 pt Liberation Sans (Arial's metrics), margins of at least
  0.5 in, no headers, footers or page numbers. Do not change these in
  `lib/nih.typ`.
- A letter's `body:` in `grant.yaml` is Typst markup; `#pi` and `#G` are
  available in it.
- Check limits and budget rules against the funding opportunity (`nofo:`),
  which can override the defaults here.

## Building

`make` builds every attachment into `out/`; `make out/strategy.pdf` builds
one; `make full` assembles the whole application into `out/full.pdf`, opened
by a checklist page and marking what is still missing; `make budget` writes
the budget into `out/budget.xlsx` (into the institution's own workbook when
its brand layer adds `budget-excel.yaml`), and `make budget-import` reads a
filled-in one back. They need `typst` 0.15 on PATH and python3 with pypdf,
openpyxl and ruamel.yaml (the build sandbox has them).

## Git

If you work in a clone of the project's repository, commit and push as
usual: the hub merges your change into the live text. If you work in the
hub's own folder (`<data folder>/<project>/`), edit files only and never run git
there: that history belongs to the hub.
