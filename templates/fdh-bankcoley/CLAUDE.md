# Florida DOH Bankhead-Coley application

A Typst project for a Bankhead-Coley Cancer Research Program proposal, edited
live in tydig. Each online PeerNet text box is one `.typ` file: paste its prose
back into PeerNet. The uploaded PDFs are built here.

## Where things are

- `grant.yaml`: every fact stated more than once (title, people, aims,
  objectives, keywords, the work plan, figure legends). Change a fact here,
  never in the `.typ` files.
- `main.typ`: Specific Aims, Research/Project Plan and References in one
  document so `@citations` resolve. `make` cuts it at the `aims`, `strategy`
  and `references` sections; keep those keys and their order.
- One attachment / text box each: `summary.typ` (general audience abstract),
  `relevance.typ`, `abstract.typ` (scientific), `impact.typ`, `workplan.typ`
  (Exhibit A), `figures.typ`, `resources.typ`, `coi.typ`, `biosketch.typ`.
- `lib/fdh.typ`: format; `lib/grant.typ`: `section`, `todo`, `letter`,
  `timeline`; `lib/brand.typ`: colours.
- `refs.bib`: references; cite as `@key` in `main.typ` only.
- `budget.yaml`: budget inputs; `lib/budget.typ` computes every total. The
  cap is 15% indirect on modified total direct costs.
- `out/`, `build/`: generated by `make`; never edit or commit.

## Build

`make` (Typst 0.15+). `make full` assembles the whole application behind its
checklist. `make budget` writes the budget workbook. The whole pipeline also
runs in a container: `just` or `podman compose up`.

## Rules

- Paths start with `/` (`image("/assets/fig1.svg")`).
- PeerNet abstracts are NOT editable once awarded: get them right.
- Indirect costs are capped at 15% of modified total direct costs.
