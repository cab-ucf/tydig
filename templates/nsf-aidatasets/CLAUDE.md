# NSF proposal: AI Datasets (NSF 26-512)

A Typst project for an NSF 26-512 proposal, Unlocking Dataset Value for
AI-Enabled Scientific Discovery, under PAPPG 24-1 (`nofo:` in
`grant.yaml`). It is edited live by people in tydig while you work, so keep
each change small and focused.

## Where things are

- `grant.yaml`: every fact stated more than once (title, people, aims,
  program, collaborators). Change a fact here, never in the `.typ` files.
  `program.measures:` lists NSF 26-512's measures of success.
- `main.typ`: the Project Description and References Cited in one document,
  so `@citations` resolve; `make` cuts it where References Cited starts, so
  keep the section keys (`description`, `references`). Its sections are the
  solicitation's, in its words and order: 1. Vision and community benefit
  for dataset enhancement, 2. Research plan, 3. Dataset governance and
  integrity, 4. Dataset availability and infrastructure, and for a Flagship
  5. Flagship Justification. Keep their headings as they are, "Broader
  Impacts" a heading on its own line, and Results from Prior NSF Support
  (at most 5 of the 15 pages) last.
- The track is the title's prefix, `mechanism.prefix:` in `grant.yaml`:
  `AIDatasets:Impact:`, `AIDatasets:Flagship:` or `AIDatasets:Planning:`
  (the solicitation's own "AIDatesets" is a typo). Set `cap:` to match:
  $2,000,000, $5,000,000 or $200,000. A Flagship adds section 5. The
  solicitation does not say whether Planning follows PAPPG II.F.1 (8 pages,
  a program officer's concurrence): ask aidatasets@nsf.gov, and set
  `limits:` to match.
- `datasets:` in `grant.yaml`: the existing datasets enhanced, tabulated in
  section 1. `aims:` are the enhancements, one each in section 2.
- `keywords:` in `grant.yaml`: 2 to 5, most important first, naming the
  directorates or scientific communities that benefit; `summary.typ` makes
  them its last line, "Keywords:" and semicolons, as NSF 26-512 asks.
- `#todo[...]` (grey) says what a passage must cover. Replace it with the
  text; never leave one in a finished section.
- `summary.typ` (Overview, Intellectual Merit, Broader Impacts: keep the
  three headings, and the Keywords line last), `budget.typ`,
  `facilities.typ` (no dollar figures), `mentoring.typ`, `letters.typ`
  (NSF's one sentence, nothing more): one upload each.
- The Data Management and Sharing Plan is written in Research.gov's DMSP
  tool, not here.
- `lib/nsf.typ`: PAPPG formatting; `lib/grant.typ`: the `section`, `letter`,
  `todo` and `timeline` helpers.
- `refs.bib`: references, every author listed; cite them as `@key` in
  `main.typ` only.
- `package.yaml`: every component of the proposal and where it comes from.
  The Checklist panel and `make full` read it.
- `budget.yaml`: the budget's inputs, each list one entry a year; each
  cost's `category:` is its Research.gov line (one that is none goes under
  G.6 Other, marked red). `lib/budget.typ` computes every total and warns in
  red where a rule is broken (senior personnel over 2 months a year, a
  subaward over its share, the award over its cap), a list's years do not
  match `years:`, or an investigator is not in it by the same name (so their
  months go unchecked); never type a total into a `.typ` file.
- `out/` (built PDFs) and `build/`: generated by `make`; never edit or commit them.

## Rules

- No web addresses in the Project Description (they show red).
- No cost sharing anywhere, in the budget or in the text.
- Budget travel to an annual in-person meeting (a PI meeting, NSF's NAIRR
  community meeting, or one in the discipline); twice-yearly meetings are
  virtual.
- Paths start with `/` (`image("/assets/x.svg")`).
- Page limits come from `limits:` in `grant.yaml` and the `section`s; over
  one, a red warning shows on its first page: shorten the text, never the
  formatting.
- PAPPG format: 11 pt Arial (Liberation Sans has its metrics, so pages count
  the same; render the final PDF where Arial is installed), 1 in margins, at
  most 6 lines an inch, no page numbers. Do not change these in `lib/nsf.typ`.
- Label simulated figures as simulations, not data.
- Check everything against the solicitation (`nofo:`) and the program page,
  which can add to the PAPPG.

## Building

`make` builds every upload into `out/`; `make full` assembles the whole
proposal into `out/full.pdf`, opened by a checklist page and marking what is
still missing; `make budget` writes the budget into `out/budget.xlsx`. They
need `typst` 0.15 on PATH and python3 with pypdf, openpyxl and ruamel.yaml
(the build sandbox has them).

## Git

If you work in a clone of the project's repository, commit and push as
usual: the hub merges your change into the live text. If you work in the
hub's own folder (`<data folder>/<project>/`), edit files only and never run
git there: that history belongs to the hub.
